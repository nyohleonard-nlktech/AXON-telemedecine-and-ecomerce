Who We Are

Built in Bamenda.

AxonForge is a student-engineers-led autonomous drone startup from NAHPI, University of Bamenda.

Our pentacopter runs Arduino Mega 2560 + MultiWii for flight control, Raspberry Pi Zero W for autonomous GPS intelligence, and ESP32 for WiFi telemetry. Sensors: 5× ultrasonic, NEO-6M GPS, MPU6050, barometer, rain, temperature. Designed and built by engineering students across 9 divisions.

Common Questions

FAQ

How does GPS delivery work?
+
When you tap "Share My GPS", your phone sends exact lat/lng coordinates. The drone navigates to those coordinates — accurate to within a few metres. No more "near the big mango tree".
How does escrow protect my payment?
+
Your payment goes to AxonForge first — not the vendor. Once you confirm receipt, we release the money to the vendor. If delivery fails, you get a full refund. You can never lose money to a dishonest vendor.
Minimum order value for drone delivery?
+
Drone delivery is available for orders valued at 3,000 XAF and above. Standard rider delivery is available for smaller items.
How do I sell my products on AxonForge?
+
Register as a vendor, submit your ID for verification, and our admin team confirms your account. Once active, list your products and we handle delivery logistics. A 3% commission applies per sale.
What are the operating hours?
+
AxonForge operates during defined daily hours. Orders outside hours are queued. 24/7 operations planned for a future version once the full drone fleet is deployed.
